Rendering modes

How an icon renders depends on whether it lives in the offline subset:

  • In the subset — rendered as an inline <svg> on the server. No @defer needed and no hydration gap.

  • Not in the subset — rendered through the <iconify-icon> web component, resolving from the Iconify CDN at runtime.

  • [forceCdn]="true" — skips the subset lookup and always renders <iconify-icon> from the CDN, even when the icon exists in the subset (useful for icons deliberately kept out of the generated subset). The scanner also EXCLUDES any icon referenced with forceCdn, so regenerating drops it from icon-subset.ts.
